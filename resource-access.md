### **Lab Email**

Lab members should have access to the CCM email (ccm.lab@ubc.ca) through their UBC account. **If you do not have access, let lab manager know.**

***Note:** As of May 2025 the lab email is not migrated, but yours (as a student) may be. This creates problems where you **won't have permission to send emails from the shared inbox**. This is easily resolvable, but IT is very slow; instead of opening a ticket, just **call IT** and they'll have it fixed in 10-ish minutes.*

#### **Instructions for accessing the email:**

**Step 1:** **Log into your [UBC Faculty and Staff email](https://outlook.office.com/mail/).** This should be in the format firstname.lastname@ubc.ca**, and you should log in with your CWL password. 

 **Step 2:** Once you are in your email, **click on the top right corner where your name and avatar are displayed**. You will see a hyperlink that says "Open another mailbox."

**Step 3:** **Click "Open another mailbox."** This will open a dialogue box. 

**Step 4:** **Type "ccm"** and the email ccm.lab@ubc.ca should pop up. **Click that account and then click "Open".**
 
 **Step 5:** **Hopefully, you are now in the inbox!**

** *(If you have a common name, and trying firstname.lastname@ubc.ca tells you that you have an incorrect password, you may have to try putting numbers after, such as first.last1@ubc.ca).*
