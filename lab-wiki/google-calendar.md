# **Google Calendar**

All lab members should have access to this; if you don't, let lab manager or Hyosub know!

-----

### **Colour coding**

The main colour of the calendar is **dark blue**, but to make things easier different types of events have different colours.

#### **Colour coding guide**

| Colour | What it means | Examples |
| ------ | ------ | ----- |
| Dark blue | Basic lab information | Lab member shifts, vacations etc. |
| Yellow | Meetings | Lab meetings, one-on-one meetings |
| Flamingo | Cool stuff outside of the lab that lab members might be interested in attending | Workshops, faculty events, etc.
| Other | Researchers may choose a colour to represent their testing sessions | Currently **Jack's studies are in green** and **Jeremy's studies are in purple**.

Having all this information on the calendar will make sure that we can best avoid booking participants in for times that a researcher or study room might not be available.
