### **Updating the List**

Feel free to add friends to the list, making sure to fill out all the columns. Other than that, there are other means through which we may have to update the list:

#### **When contacting participants**

We want to be able to keep tabs on who we have contacted for what. When you've emailed a participant (whether it be initial outreach, confirming a visit, sending a reminder, etc), update the **Last contacted** column. 

#### **When a participant comes in**

We've only recently started keeping track of potential participants' gender, and for many we do not have information on handedness. This type of information may be useful in the long-run, for instance when we want to keep an even split between the sex of participants in an experiment. 

When a participant comes in for a visit, there is information that we will collect from them - make a note of it and update the database!

Also, we may get participants that are not yet on the potential participants list - When participants come in and complete an experiment, ask if they are interested in coming back! And if they are, make sure they are in the database. If a participant is in the database and there are columns (i.e. handedness, gender, etc) that are not filled out, fill them out!

**In summary, update the following if applicable:**
 - Gender
 - Handedness
 - Add participant to database

#### **Exclusion criteria**

**Keep in mind some of our exclusion criteria** - some of our studies require that participants have no known history of neurological impairment, for instance. At the moment we can't store this kind of sensitive information on a Google Sheet (which isn't super secure), but if a participant may be ineligible **make a note on the participant list along the lines of "confirm eligibility"**.
